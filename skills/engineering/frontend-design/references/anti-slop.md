# Anti-Slop Review

Repeated patterns prompt questions about intent. The brief, real content and existing product decide whether they are appropriate.

## Find the missing decision

- Stock heroes, two calls to action and identical cards may conceal weak hierarchy. Organize around the actual task and evidence.
- Purple gradients, cream-and-terracotta editorial styling, black-and-neon palettes and newspaper rules can be deliberate or a new default. Explain their product connection.
- Nested cards, pills, ornaments, shadows and uniform radii may obscure grouping. Use type, spacing and surfaces to express relationships.
- Numbering belongs to sequences; real output replaces invented statistics/testimonials.
- Fonts need readable script coverage and appropriate roles, not novelty or blacklists. Check Chinese/Latin fallback, availability and wrapping.
- Motion explains causality or realizes a creative direction. Frequent actions feel immediate; reduced-motion and keyboard use stay usable.
- Copy names the user's object, action and recovery. Remove generic enthusiasm, filler labels and unsupported claims.

## Repair

Name the rendered defect, its impact and the boundary to preserve. Compare a relevant reference/sibling. Fix the shared cause before adding decoration, replacing fonts or introducing a component. Recheck affected consumers and states. A lower scan count does not establish better design.
